``` ### What changed The **Data Coverage → Show coverage** layer is now interactive. When it's turned on: * 🟢 **Click a green polygon** → popup with its landcover type, TCODE, and description. * 🖱️ **Hover over a polygon** → it becomes slightly darker/thicker. * ⬜ **Unknown/Unsurveyed areas** aren't part of the layer, so they remain uncovered. * 🔍 Your normal address/coordinate search still works independently. One caveat: because the 8 MB file is being displayed as its original polygons, clicking a very tiny polygon can sometimes require zooming in. That's preferable here to doing a huge dissolve operation on every page load.
